# Economics

## User Value

If a startup saves `$500/mo`, that is `$6,000/year`. For a seed-stage team, that can cover a design contractor, cloud experiments, or an extra month of several SaaS subscriptions.

## Business Model Options

- Free self-serve audit for lead generation.
- `$499` fixed cleanup sprint for high-savings teams.
- `$99/mo` monitoring plan after billing integrations exist.
- Partner channel with accelerators and startup CFO firms.

## Cost Structure

- LLM summary calls are small because the audit result is compact.
- Supabase and Resend costs remain low at early volume.
- The largest future cost is integration maintenance, not compute.

## Example Unit Economics

If 1,000 monthly audits convert 6% into consultation calls and 20% of calls buy a `$499` sprint, monthly revenue is about `$5,988`. The product can stay lightweight until integrations justify a subscription tier.
