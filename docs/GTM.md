# GTM

## Audience

Seed to Series A startups with 5-80 employees, especially teams that adopted AI tools bottom-up across engineering, content, support, and product.

## Positioning

Credex is the fastest way to turn AI tool sprawl into a board-ready savings plan.

## Launch Motion

- Product Hunt launch with anonymized example audits.
- Founder-led LinkedIn posts showing before/after AI stacks.
- Free audit link for startup communities and accelerators.
- "AI renewal checklist" lead magnet for finance and ops teams.

## Sales Wedge

High-savings audits over `$500/mo` trigger a consultation CTA. The first paid service could be a fixed-price AI spend cleanup sprint.

## Differentiation

Most SaaS spend tools focus on large enterprise procurement. Credex starts with AI-specific tool overlap and speaks to startups that need immediate runway wins.
